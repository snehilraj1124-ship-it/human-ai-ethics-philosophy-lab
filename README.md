# Human-AI Ethics & Philosophy Lab

> An interdisciplinary analytics project exploring human-AI ethics, responsible AI, philosophical frameworks, ethical alignment, and AI governance using data analytics and machine learning.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

**Human-AI Ethics & Philosophy Lab** explores how Artificial Intelligence systems interact with human values, ethical principles, decision-making, and governance.

The project combines:

- Artificial Intelligence
- Data Analytics
- Machine Learning
- Philosophy
- Responsible AI
- AI Governance
- Decision Science
- Human-AI Interaction

A synthetic dataset containing **1,000 simulated AI decision cases** is analyzed across multiple application domains including healthcare, finance, education, employment, transportation, and media.

The purpose of the project is not to determine whether an AI decision is objectively "moral." Instead, it demonstrates how ethical concepts can be structured into analytical dimensions and examined using quantitative methods.

---

# 🎯 Objectives

The major objectives are:

1. Analyze ethical dimensions of AI systems.
2. Study human-AI alignment.
3. Examine fairness, privacy, autonomy, accountability, and explainability.
4. Analyze the role of human oversight in AI decision-making.
5. Study potential benefits and risks associated with AI systems.
6. Build analytical Responsible AI and Ethical Tension indices.
7. Apply statistical analysis and machine learning.
8. Explore AI governance patterns.
9. Connect quantitative analysis with philosophical reasoning.
10. Demonstrate responsible interpretation of AI ethics data.

# 🧠 Philosophical Frameworks

The project uses multiple philosophical perspectives to interpret AI decision-making.

## 1. Deontological Ethics

Deontological ethics emphasizes:

- Duties
- Rights
- Rules
- Responsibilities
- Moral constraints

From this perspective, an AI system should respect fundamental principles even when violating them could produce a desirable outcome.

---

## 2. Utilitarian Ethics

Utilitarianism focuses on consequences and evaluates decisions according to their overall benefits and harms.

Important analytical dimensions include:

- Human benefit
- Potential harm
- Utility
- Social impact
- Outcome optimization

---

## 3. Virtue Ethics

Virtue ethics focuses on responsible behavior and characteristics such as:

- Fairness
- Responsibility
- Honesty
- Transparency
- Human-centered decision-making

---

## 4. Justice Ethics

Justice-oriented approaches emphasize:

- Fairness
- Equality
- Equal treatment
- Distribution of benefits and risks
- Protection from discrimination

---

## 5. Human Agency

The project also examines **human agency** in AI-assisted decisions.

Key questions include:

- Who makes the final decision?
- Can humans override the AI?
- How much autonomy does the user retain?
- Is human oversight meaningful?
- Does AI assistance reduce or support human decision-making?

# 📋 Dataset Variables

| Variable | Description |
|---|---|
| `case_id` | Unique synthetic case identifier |
| `ai_system` | Type of AI system |
| `domain` | Application domain |
| `human_oversight` | Level of human oversight |
| `explainability_score` | Explainability score, 0–100 |
| `fairness_score` | Fairness score, 0–100 |
| `privacy_score` | Privacy protection score, 0–100 |
| `autonomy_score` | Human autonomy score, 0–100 |
| `accountability_score` | Accountability score, 0–100 |
| `human_benefit_score` | Estimated human benefit, 0–100 |
| `harm_risk_score` | Potential harm risk, 0–100 |
| `trust_score` | Human trust in AI, 0–100 |
| `human_agreement_score` | Agreement between human and AI outcomes |
| `oversight_score` | Numerical representation of human oversight |
| `human_ai_alignment_index` | Composite alignment measure |
| `responsible_ai_index` | Composite Responsible AI measure |
| `ethical_tension_index` | Composite ethical tension measure |
| `ai_role` | Role of AI in the decision process |
| `preferred_governance` | Exploratory governance category |
